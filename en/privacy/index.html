Privacy Policy

Prelaunch template: review legal text for your actual operator identity, jurisdiction, hosting, analytics and advertising setup before publishing.

Noreqia Tools is designed to minimize data collection. The current browser-based tools process user-entered text and selected files locally whenever the tool supports it.

This prelaunch build does not include analytics or advertising code. If advertising or analytics are enabled later, this policy must be updated to identify providers, cookies or local storage used, purposes, retention, and user choices.

Language preference may be stored locally by the browser or inferred from the URL. Third-party resources, such as the QR library CDN, may receive standard network request data such as IP address and user agent.

Contact requests sent by email are handled by the user's and recipient's email providers.
